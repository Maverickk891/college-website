ABOUT OUR INSTITUTE

A place to discover what you're capable of.

Established in 1999 under the Kodava Education Society, Coorg Institute of Technology is located in Ponnampet, Kodagu. CIT aims to provide quality technical education in an environment that encourages learning and growth.
